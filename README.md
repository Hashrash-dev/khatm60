# 60-Day Quran Khatm

Read and listen to the whole Quran in 60 days: half a juz (one hizb) a day, split into two sittings.

- 604-page Madani mushaf pages, built in
- Verse-by-verse recitation with a choice of reciters (via the [Al Quran Cloud](https://alquran.cloud) API)
- Pages follow the recitation; speed, verse repeat and Bismillah options
- Progress tracking for all 60 days, saved on the device
- Installable on phones and works offline (pages you have opened)

Live app: https://hashrash-dev.github.io/khatm60/
